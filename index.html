BoBOTs

BoBOTs

"BoBOTs" is a party local multiplayer game in a world of toys where you dash and bounce your way to victory through different minigames! In this project I worked primarily on the local multiplayer system, UI, the Bash Out gamemode and the AI Bots to fill the empty spots. We were only 4 programmers for this game, but I'm really proud of our effort and the game that came out thanks that.

Impacto a la NADA

Impacto a la NADA

"Impacto a la NADA" is a puzzle VR game where a scientist working at the "NADA" institution finds a planetary simulator and they'll have to controll the forces between different celestial bodies to move an asteroid to its final destination. During the development of this prototype, I worked in the sound system VR interactions with the planets and the simulation, translating the core gameplay of the puzzles to something understadable and intuitive with the VR limitations. This project was made by 5 programmers using Unity during 4 months where we also had to cover for the missing roles like game design and 3D art. Despite that, I think the gameplay shows the potencial the game had.
